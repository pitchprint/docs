---
title: "Introduction"
description: "PitchPrint is the product customizer that turns your print store into a self-serve design studio."
---

**Let your customers design it themselves.** PitchPrint turns your print website into an easy-to-use, Photoshop-style editor — shoppers personalize business cards, flyers, calendars, apparel, mugs and more, and you receive a print-ready PDF of exactly what they created.

No Photoshop skills needed, for you or your customers. It runs as a hosted service, so the heavy lifting happens on our servers and updates roll out automatically.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add PitchPrint to your store">
    Install the plugin for your platform in a few clicks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your designs">
    Build customizable templates — fonts, colors, images and editable areas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your customer personalizes it">
    Shoppers open the editor on your product page and make the design their own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get print-ready files">
    Receive a print-ready PDF and proof on checkout, then send it straight to print.
  </Step>
</Steps>
