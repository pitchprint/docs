## Your customers design it. You just print it.

PitchPrint is a **web-to-print product customizer** that plugs into your online store. Your customers open a design editor right on the product page, personalize what they're buying — business cards, flyers, mugs, t-shirts, banners, photo books, phone cases — and check out. You receive a print-ready PDF of exactly what they designed, ready to send to production.

Nobody needs design software. Your customers work in a simple drag-and-drop editor in the browser; you work in a hosted admin dashboard. There's no server to maintain, and updates roll out automatically.

More than 2,000 print companies worldwide run their storefronts on PitchPrint.

## What's inside

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The design editor" icon="palette">
    A browser-based editor with text, images, shapes, masks, backgrounds, QR codes and text art. Customers can upload their own logos and photos, snap elements to guides, and work on multi-page products like brochures and photo books.

    Elements you don't want touched can be locked in place, so branding, legal text and trim marks survive contact with customers.

    Available in 35 languages, and every label in the interface can be reworded to match how you talk about your products.

    <Card title="Explore the editor" icon="arrow-right" href="/tutorial/design-editor" horizontal>
      A tour of the tools your customers will use.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Three ways to show it on your product page" icon="window-maximize">
    Pick the display mode that suits each product. **Modal** opens the editor full-screen for a focused design session — this is the default. **Inline** embeds the full editor directly in the product page so customization stays part of the shopping flow. **Mini** is a compact in-page editor for simple personalization like a name or a short message.

    <Frame caption="Modal, mini and inline display modes">
      <img src="https://pitchprint.com/assets/img/use/modal.png" alt="Modal display mode" />
    </Frame>

    <Card title="Display modes" icon="arrow-right" href="/tutorial/display-modes" horizontal>
      Choose and configure each mode.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="AI tools your customers can use" icon="sparkles">
    **AI Imaginate** turns a text prompt into artwork in seconds, so customers with no design instincts still get something they like. **Remove Background** isolates a subject cleanly for apparel and product prints. **Upscale** rescues low-resolution uploads before they reach production. **Sketch to Image** builds finished artwork from a rough drawing, **Outpainting** extends an image to fill a larger canvas, and **Smart Replace** swaps out objects inside an image.

    <Frame caption="Generating artwork from a text prompt inside the editor">
      <img src="https://pitchprint.com/assets/img/use/ai-generator.gif" alt="AI image generation in the editor" />
    </Frame>

    <Card title="AI tools" icon="arrow-right" href="/tutorial/ai-image-generator" horizontal>
      Enable and configure each AI feature.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Interactive 3D preview" icon="cube">
    Customers rotate, zoom and inspect a realistic 3D render of their design on the finished product before ordering — the single most effective way to stop "that's not what I expected" arriving after the print run.

    Use ready-made models from our store, or bring your own mesh into **3D Studio** and map your product's artwork onto it yourself.

    <Card title="3D views" icon="arrow-right" href="/documentation/3d-view-introduction" horizontal>
      Set up 3D previews and UV mapping.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Modules for real print products" icon="puzzle-piece">
    Modules handle the awkward parts of specific product categories, so you're not rebuilding the editor for every SKU.

    - **Data Form** — a quick-edit form instead of canvas editing, built for text-heavy items like business cards and banners. Logo and photo uploads drop straight into the right position.
    - **Canvas Adjuster** — customers pick preset or custom dimensions for large-format prints, and your product price updates as the size changes.
    - **Page Loader** — add, swap or delete pages from a source design. Made for magazines, menus, programmes and brochures.
    - **Template Color** — show a design on different product colors without leaving the editor. Essential for apparel and casings.
    - **Swatch** and **Single/Double-Sided** — offer material or finish choices and per-side pricing.
    - **Variable Data** and **Shuffle** — populate a design from a data source, or randomize placement across copies.

    <Frame caption="The Data Form module in a business card product">
      <img src="https://pitchprint.com/assets/img/use/data-form-new.png" alt="Data Form module" />
    </Frame>

    <Card title="All modules" icon="arrow-right" href="/tutorial/design-selector-module" horizontal>
      What each module does and how to enable it.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Layouts, smart sizes and styling" icon="brush">
    **Layouts** let you decide which tools appear for which products — a stripped-back layout for quick business card edits, the full toolkit for a magazine. **Smart Sizes** spin one design into portrait, landscape and square variants without rebuilding it. There are dedicated mobile layouts too, so the editor stays usable on a phone.

    Beyond that, you can theme the app with your own CSS, add custom JavaScript, and edit the HTML to strip out anything you don't sell.

    <Frame caption="One design, resized into multiple layouts">
      <img src="https://pitchprint.com/assets/img/use/smart-resize.png" alt="Smart Sizes" />
    </Frame>

    <Card title="Layouts" icon="arrow-right" href="/tutorial/layouts-v10" horizontal>
      Build and assign layouts to designs.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fonts, images and assets" icon="font">
    Search and use **Google Fonts** directly in the editor, or upload your own TTF and OTF files — TTF is used for print-ready PDF output, WOFF for fast loading in the browser. You can also upload SVG shapes and build your own clipart library.

    Your image gallery is keyword-searchable by customers, and **Pixabay** is built in, giving them over a million royalty-free photos to work with.

    <Card title="Adding fonts" icon="arrow-right" href="/tutorial/how-to-add-your-own-fonts" horizontal>
      Upload fonts and enable Google Fonts.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Print-ready output" icon="file-pdf">
    Every order produces a high-resolution vector PDF. Text and shapes render as true vectors; images render at their original resolution. Output in **CMYK with spot colors** or RGB, with support for layered elements and overprint — and JPEG or PNG proofs alongside.

    You can tune rendering with Ghostscript arguments, run a resolution check to catch low-quality customer uploads before they print, and automate delivery of finished files straight to your printer.

    <Frame caption="High-resolution, print-ready vector output">
      <img src="https://pitchprint.com/assets/img/use/transp-high-res.png" alt="Print-ready vector PDF output" />

    <Card title="PDF rendering" icon="arrow-right" href="/tutorial/pdf-rendering" horizontal>
      Color modes, bleed and output settings.
    </Card>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Spark — print automation without the editor" icon="bolt">
    Spark is an API that generates print-ready PDFs in bulk, no editor involved. Send customer details and it returns unique files with the text and images swapped per copy.

    It's built for estate agents refreshing business cards, event planners ordering per-client banners, direct mail campaigns, and companies producing named gifts for every employee. Three steps from request to file on the printer.

    <Frame caption="Spark generating personalized print files via API">
      <img src="https://pitchprint.com/assets/img/use/spark-new.png" alt="Spark print automation API" />

    <Card title="Spark" icon="arrow-right" href="/tutorial/spark" horizontal>

  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Order handling and integrations" icon="link">
    When an order comes in you get the project details, a print-ready PDF, and a screenshot proof of what the customer designed — with the option to load the project back into the editor and adjust it yourself.

    Designs and previews can be attached to your order confirmation emails, and **webhooks** notify your own systems the moment a project is saved. Everything runs on AWS serverless infrastructure, so traffic spikes are somebody else's problem.
