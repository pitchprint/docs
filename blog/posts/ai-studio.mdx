A few months ago, we introduced the **AI Tab** within the editor on the user side, enabling end users to generate images directly from text prompts. Since its release, we’ve received an overwhelming number of requests to expand on this functionality and make it more accessible to store owners and administrators.

In response to this feedback, we’re excited to announce the launch of our **AI Studio**—a powerful new feature available within the admin panel. The AI Studio allows administrators to generate high-quality image resources simply by entering descriptive text prompts. This makes it easier than ever to create custom graphics, backgrounds, or product visuals on the fly, without the need for third-party tools or graphic design experience.

This new tool provides added flexibility and creative control, helping you streamline your workflow and enhance the design experience for your customers.

## How to Get Started

To use the AI Studio, log in to your admin account and go to the **AI Studio** section. Click on the **Create New** button to begin.

## Steps to Generate an Image

1. **Enter a Text Prompt** – Describe the image you want to create.
2. **Select a Size** – Choose the image dimensions.
3. **Select a Style** – Pick a visual style, then click Generate.

## AI Studio Feature Overview

-   **Use Template** – Start with a previously created template as your prompt. This is ideal for reusing or editing existing prompts to streamline your workflow.
-   **Use Prompt** – Enter a custom text prompt from scratch to generate a new image tailored to your specific needs.

## Editing Tools Explained

-   ## Remove Background

    Instantly removes the image background.

-   ## Upscale

    Improves image resolution and quality.

-   ## Use as Template

    Reopens the editor using the same prompt to create a new version.

-   ## Add to Resource

    Save the image as a Picture or Background for your customers to use.

-   ## Download

    Save the image to your device.

-   ## Image Details

    Displays information about the image style and settings used.
