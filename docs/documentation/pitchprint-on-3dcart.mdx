---
title: "PitchPrint on 3DCart"
description: "One-click install of PitchPrint on a 3DCart store, then assign designs and download print files."
---

[3DCart](https://3dcart.com) is a hosted ecommerce framework similar to Shopify, with a strong focus on SEO and conversion. PitchPrint installs in one click.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Open the [PitchPrint listing on the 3DCart app store](https://apps.3dcart.com/pitchprint-product-customizer.html), click the green **Install App** button, enter your secure store URL and proceed.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5e65e78104286364bc96659a/file-8d3dhWcffb.png" alt="Installing PitchPrint from the 3DCart app store" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Get your login details">
    PitchPrint installs itself and emails your login credentials to your 3DCart admin email address.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your design templates">
    On the [Designs page](https://admin.pitchprint.io/designs), build templates from scratch or import them from our design store.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign designs to products">
    On the [Products page](https://admin.pitchprint.io/products), hover over a product and pick a design from the dropdown (a business card design for the business card product you sell, for example).

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5e65eb6004286364bc9665a1/file-XT8GrUumU3.png" alt="Assigning a design to a 3DCart product" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Place a test order">
    Open that product on your storefront and look for the **Customize Product** button. Clicking it launches the editor with your assigned template.

    <Tip>
      If the button does not appear, get in touch and we'll take a look.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the print files">
    Grab the print-ready PDF from the [Orders page](https://admin.pitchprint.io/orders). You can also reload the project there to modify it and regenerate the file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you are new to the platform, [Getting Started with PitchPrint](/docs/tutorial/getting-started-with-pitchprint) walks through the concepts and workflow in full.
</Note>
