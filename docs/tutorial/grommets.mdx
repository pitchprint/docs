---
title: "Grommets"
description: "Add reinforced eyelet holes to banners and signage by entering a Grommet spacing tag in the page settings of a design."
---

Grommets are metal or plastic eyelets inserted into pre-punched holes in printed materials. They reinforce the hole to prevent abrasion and tearing, giving your products greater durability and ease of installation.

This feature is especially useful for products such as banners, signage, flags, posters, canvas prints, and other items where hanging or securing with cords is required.

Grommets can be added to the canvas by inserting a **tag directly into the page** that defines the horizontal and vertical spacing values.

Your tag should be written as follows:

Grommet: 2 x 2

-   The first value (**2**) represents the **Horizontal spacing**.
-   The second value (**2**) represents the **Vertical spacing**.

These values correspond to the measurement units defined in your product setup (e.g., inches, centimeters, millimeters).

### To Enable Grommets

Follow the steps below:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your design">
    Open your **Design** in PitchPrint.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Page Settings">
    Click on **Page Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Tags section">
    Locate the **Tags** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the grommet tag">
    Enter your grommet tag as shown below.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/69a6e59512f11a8a9d61bf48/file-2pItjvSyjQ.png" alt="Page settings Tags section with a Grommet spacing tag entered" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Note:**

-   **Horizontal values:** Any value may be entered.
-   **Vertical values:** Only the values **1** or **2** are permitted.
-   **Synchronization:** Any changes made at the top of the page will automatically be applied to the bottom of the page as well.

Save your design, and you’re all set! If you encounter any issues, please contact us at **support@pitchprint.com**
