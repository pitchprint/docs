---
title: "How to enable Pixabay image search"
description: "Switch on the Pixabay plugin in your admin settings so customers can search millions of free stock images inside the editor."
---

[Pixabay](https://pixabay.com) is an image library with a great collection of free images. They also allow external sites to search through their collection and as such, we have integrated PitchPrint with their service such that your customers can easily search for images within the app and use them freely in their designs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the Pixabay plugin">
    To enable this feature navigate to the [Settings page](https://admin.pitchprint.com/settings). Go to the Plugins section and enable the Pixabay Search feature as shown in the image below, please save the changes you make.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/637b7bb1fd962f4d057bb5c7/file-QPPsHIn8fH.png" alt="Plugins section of the admin settings with Pixabay Search switched on" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the tab in the app">
    You will then see the Pixabay Search tab when opening PitchPrint on your site as shown in the image below:

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/637b7c27bedde91b3d8c97db/file-K3SNVd5yA3.png" alt="Pixabay Search tab open in the PitchPrint app" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
