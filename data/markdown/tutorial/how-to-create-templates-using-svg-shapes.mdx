From this article you will learn how to create a design template using an SVG shape.

\[Lets use a cellphone case as example.\]

**Part 1: Creating the SVG**

1\. You will need to design the shape on the phone cover, as seen below:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/59e46df0042863379ddcb128/file-IKT7d1nVl6.png)

2\. Now you can save this design as an SVG - ( [http://docs.pitchprint.com/article/69-how-to-save-your-svgs-for-use-in-pitchprint](http://docs.pitchprint.com/article/69-how-to-save-your-svgs-for-use-in-pitchprint))

**Part 2: Creating the design template on PitchPrint**

1\. Select/create a category for your design and then click on **New Design**:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/6527b69e25f843047502428e/file-UnzRRh3J4S.png)

2\. Next you will have to give the design a name and then click **SUBMIT**:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/6527b6cc25f843047502428f/file-O4vqLTSS6l.png)

3\. Now you need to set up your canvas:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/6527b7011ec9493542047a90/file-tiKj2adwy7.png)

4\. Now you can upload your SVG onto PitchPrint, Once its upload you can just click on it and it will appear on your canvas:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/6527b73c74982009a94e6fb6/file-fLrTWNZ7Nb.png)

NOTE: By selecting the SVG on the canvas you can check the sizing and setting in the sidebar.

5\. The last two things you will need to do: is to put Transform Lock on (so that the client can't move the shape) and to put Clickable Image Mask on (Now your client can insert their design into the shape)

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/6527b7de74982009a94e6fb7/file-8TqbysGROw.png)
