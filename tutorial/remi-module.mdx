The Remix Module is a simple tool that randomly fills out mask shapes with pictures from customer’s gallery.

<Tip>
  You can see a sample here:  [Photo prints demo](https://wp.demo.pitchprint.io/product/photo-prints/)

  Select the My Pictures Tab or Facebook / Instagram tabs and you will find the Magic wand icon if you have multiple photos there. Click to shuffle the photos
</Tip>

It is a simple button that appears on the Upload, Facebook and Instagram Photo tabs and when clicked, uses the photos in that collection to fill out any mask shape that has a chain value  **remix** across all the pages in the current design.

Enabling Remix Module in your designs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a design">
    To use this feature, simply [edit your design or create a new one](https://admin.pitchprint.io/designs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a mask shape">
    Place a mask shape on the design canvas. A mask shape is a simple shape with just paths. All items in the Plain Shapes collection can be used as masks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the chain value to remix">
    Then check the Admin tab on the left and enter in the value of **“remix”** in the chain input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat for every mask">
    Repeat this process for as many masks you want to be in the collection, even across pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the Remix Module">
    Save your design and enable the Remix Module for that design in the design configuration window under Modules tab.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/654b651d38fda741c74d5900/file-TwmBIWVBbe.png" alt="Modules tab of the design configuration window with the Remix Module switched on" />
    </Frame>

    _**Please note, this Module becomes enabled ONLY if there are photos loaded in any of your collections: Upload, Facebook or Instagram**_
  </Step>
</Steps>
