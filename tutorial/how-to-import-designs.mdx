---
title: "How to import designs"
description: "Bring ready-made designs from the PitchPrint store into a category in your own store with the Import Designs button."
---

In this article, you will receive step-by-step instructions on how to import designs from the PitchPrint store to your own store.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your PitchPrint store">
    To start, first login to your PitchPrint store: https://admin.pitchprint.com/login which will take you to the dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Designs page">
    Next 'click' on **Designs** in the side panel:

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/652544c2c00c2b65208e2187/file-751SxHzVw3.png" alt="Designs item in the admin side panel" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the target category">
    Once the design page has loaded, Select the appropriate category you'd like to import the design/s into. (Lets use Canvas Prints as an example)

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/6525437f05231a0b51d238be/file-0tq16NgV2P.png" alt="Category list on the designs page with Canvas Prints selected" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Import Designs">
    Now click on the **Import Designs** button:

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/6525438c05231a0b51d238bf/file-Ngz3utJ9lm.png" alt="Import Designs button on the designs page" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a source category">
    The following window will pop-up: You can use the drop down menu, to select a category you'd like to choose a design/s from

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/61af05b764dede799a10733d/file-r2eS9Dvi9U.png" alt="Import window with a drop-down for choosing the source category" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Select and import the designs">
    Once you have chosen a category, select the designs you want to import and then 'click' the I**mport Designs** button Its as easy as that.
  </Step>
</Steps>
