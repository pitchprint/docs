---
title: "How to install PitchPrint on BigCommerce"
description: "Install the PitchPrint app from the BigCommerce App Store and assign your first design."
---

BigCommerce is an ecommerce platform built for scale. PitchPrint installs as a BigCommerce app, so setup is a few clicks.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app listing">
    Go to [PitchPrint on the BigCommerce App Store](https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/pitchprint/?search=pitchprint).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in or register">
    Click **Get This App**. You'll be prompted to log in to BigCommerce or create an account.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5f6075b7cff47e00168f6c0a/file-ZQ7IPmlg4M.png" alt="BigCommerce login prompt" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the app">
    The PitchPrint installation page loads — if it doesn't, click [the app link](https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/pitchprint/) again now that you're logged in. Click the blue **Install** button.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5f60778e52faff00174ef9b1/file-c959tX1weC.png" alt="The PitchPrint installation page" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in to the PitchPrint admin">
    Installation ends at the PitchPrint login page. Your credentials were emailed to the address you registered with BigCommerce.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5f6078434cedfd0017634935/file-1YbDwX9sBF.png" alt="The PitchPrint admin login page" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a product in BigCommerce">
    In your BigCommerce admin open **Products** and click **Add** — a business card, banner or t-shirt, for example.

    <Tip>
      In a hurry? Use one of the sample products that ships with a new BigCommerce account and skip to the next step.
    </Tip>

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5f6079504cedfd00173b7f48/file-qRxuC623l3.png" alt="Adding a product in BigCommerce" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign a design to the product">
    Open the [Products page](https://admin.pitchprint.io/products) in the PitchPrint admin, hover over your product and assign a design — the default **Lines** design is fine for a test.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5f607e69cff47e00168f6c31/file-wI3Pz70Glo.png" alt="Assigning a design to a BigCommerce product" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Test it on your storefront">
    Open the product on your store — you should see the **Customize this Design** button. Click it to launch the editor, personalize, and add to cart to complete a test order.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5f6081b3cff47e00168f6c3d/file-oldJshIZBu.png" alt="The Customize this Design button on a BigCommerce product page" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the print files">
    Open **Orders** in your PitchPrint admin to download the print-ready files for each order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Stuck at any point? Reach us by email or live chat.
</Note>
