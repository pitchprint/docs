---
title: "PitchPrint For Shopify.. Getting Started"
description: "Install the PitchPrint app on Shopify, create designs, assign them to products and download print files."
---

PitchPrint lets your Shopify customers personalize products — business cards, t-shirts, mugs, letterheads — right on the product page before adding to cart.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Create a store at [shopify.com](http://www.shopify.com/) if you don't have one, then install PitchPrint from the [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/pitchprint) — or search "PitchPrint" under **Apps** in your Shopify admin.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in to the PitchPrint admin">
    Installation takes you to [admin.pitchprint.com/login](https://admin.pitchprint.com/login). Use the credentials emailed to the address linked to your Shopify store — check your spam and promotions folders if you can't find them.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/61e51e34c73ba12e69a506d5/file-TriCR6bbZW.png" alt="The PitchPrint dashboard" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Create or import designs">
    Click **Designs** in the left panel. Build your own templates, or click **Import Designs** in the top-right of a category to pull ready-made ones from our design store.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/61e51f131adf855680c7a4a2/file-cb8gGPcPq3.png" alt="The Designs page" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign a design to a product">
    Go to [Products](https://admin.pitchprint.com/products) and hover over a product. Four options appear: **Assign a Design**, **Full Window**, **Upload** and **Required**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/61e5212079a38f5473fd46f2/file-FIBIt35chk.png" alt="Product assignment options" />
    </Frame>

    | Option | What it does |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Assign a Design** | Links a design — or a whole category, so customers pick from several — to the product. |
    | **Full Window** | Sets the [display mode](/tutorial/display-modes) for the editor. |
    | **Upload** | Lets customers upload their own artwork instead of editing your template. Green means enabled. |
    | **Required** | Forces customization — the product can't be added to cart until a design is submitted. |
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test it on your storefront">
    Open the product on your store. You should see a **Customize this design** button.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/61e526941adf855680c7a4bf/file-9PvmVoNxMO.png" alt="The Customize this design button on a Shopify product page" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the print files">
    Open the order in your Shopify admin, click the context menu in the top-right and choose **Download PitchPrint Files**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/58fe2cbf2c7d3a057f888022/file-o032x79eZP.png" alt="Download PitchPrint Files in the Shopify order menu" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Disable cookie consent apps during setup. While they're active, PitchPrint won't run until the customer has accepted all cookies.
</Warning>
