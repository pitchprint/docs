Welcome to PitchPrint! This guide takes you step-by-step from creating your account to downloading the print-ready PDF from your first customized order. No design or programming skills required.

## Step 1: Create your account and keys

Set up the account and domain that will hold your designs and issue the keys your store uses.

1. Go to [admin.pitchprint.io/register](https://admin.pitchprint.io/register) and create your free account — or [log in](https://admin.pitchprint.io/login) if you already have one.
2. Open the [Domains page](https://admin.pitchprint.io/domains) and click **Add Domain** in the top-right corner.
3. Type any name for the domain (it's just a label — you can rename it later) and click **Submit**.
4. PitchPrint provisions the domain with a sample design and generates your **API Key** and **Secret Key**.

<Info>
  The **API Key** identifies your store on the public-facing side; the **Secret Key** signs back-end requests and must be kept private — never expose it in the browser.
</Info>

## Step 3: Create/import a design

A **design** is the template your customers personalize. Create one for each product you want customized.

1. Open the [Designs page](https://admin.pitchprint.io/designs) in your PitchPrint admin.
2. To test quickly, **import** a ready-made design from our store straight into your domain — or click **Create** to build one from scratch.
3. Give the design a name and save it.

<Tip>
  Importing a sample design is the fastest way to see the full flow end-to-end before building your own.
</Tip>

## Step 4: Assign a design and place a test order

Link the design to a product, then try it as a customer would.

1. In your store admin, open (or create) a product and assign your PitchPrint design to it.
2. Visit that product on your storefront — you'll see a **Customize** button.
3. Click it to open the editor with your template loaded, personalize the design, and submit it.

## Step 5: Download your print-ready files

When a customer submits, PitchPrint generates a **project** with a print-ready PDF and a proof screenshot.

- On most platforms, download the PDF from the order in your **store admin's order page**.
- You can also grab a high-resolution raster (PNG) version of the design.

<Info>
  On **Shopify** or **3DCart**? These platforms don't allow apps on the order page, so download your files from the [Projects page](https://admin.pitchprint.io/projects) in the PitchPrint admin instead.
</Info>
