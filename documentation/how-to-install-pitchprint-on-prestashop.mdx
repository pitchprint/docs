---
title: "How To Install PitchPrint On PrestaShop"
description: "Install and configure the PitchPrint module on a PrestaShop store."
---

PrestaShop is an open source cart framework built on PHP. Follow these steps to install the PitchPrint module.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install PrestaShop">
    Download PrestaShop from the [developer versions page](https://www.prestashop.com/en/developers-versions#previous-version) and install it on your server following the instructions in the package.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download the PitchPrint module">
    Get the latest release from [github.com/pitchprint/prestashop/releases](https://github.com/pitchprint/prestashop/releases).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload the module">
    Unzip it and upload to your PrestaShop `modules` directory.

    <Warning>
      Upload the `pitchprint` folder itself, not just its contents. The path must be `modules/pitchprint`.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set upload folder permissions">
    Set `site/modules/pitchprint/uploads/files` to permission `777`.

    <Warning>
      Once installed, work through our [security measures](/tutorial/security-measures) before going live.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install and configure the module">
    In your PrestaShop admin click **Modules**, search for "pitchprint", click **Install**, then click **Configure**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate your PitchPrint keys">
    In a new tab, [create an account](https://admin.pitchprint.com/register) or [log in](https://admin.pitchprint.com/login), then add a domain on the [domains page](https://admin.pitchprint.com/domains).

    Enter your intended live domain, not a localhost address. You can still use the keys for local testing. PitchPrint returns an **API Key** and **Secret Key**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the keys in PrestaShop">
    Paste both keys into the PitchPrint configuration boxes and save. Leave the JavaScript and CSS fields empty for now.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assign a design to a product">
    Go to **Catalog → Products** and click **edit** on any product. PitchPrint sits at the bottom of the left menu. Click it, assign one of your designs and save.

    To create more designs, go to the [PitchPrint Designs page](https://admin.pitchprint.com/designs).

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/58fe2a7b2c7d3a057f887fff/file-L9DXnY14LB.png" alt="Assigning a PitchPrint design to a PrestaShop product" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Test it on your storefront">
    Open the product on your store and check for the **Customize** button.

    <Tip>
      If the button does not appear, see [troubleshooting](/tutorial/personalize-button-no-showing) or open a ticket.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect your print files">
    When an order comes in, the design download links appear against each product in that order.
  </Step>
</Steps>
