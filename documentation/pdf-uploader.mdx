Upload a PDF and PitchPrint turns it into a design you can keep editing on the canvas. <Badge icon="badge-alert" color="orange" size="sm">Beta</Badge> You do not have to rebuild artwork you already have.

## Why the uploader exists

PitchPrint can convert an uploaded PDF in two ways, and both of them lose editability:

| Conversion | What you can still edit |
| --- | --- |
| **PDF → SVG** | Colors only. Other editing is unavailable. |
| **PDF → PNG** | Nothing. Every element is flattened into a single image. |

The PDF Uploader avoids both limitations and keeps the artwork editable.

## What you can edit after uploading

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Text" icon="font">
    Modify any text element carried over from the PDF.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Groups" icon="object-group">
    Group and ungroup items to manage elements.

  <Card title="Images" icon="image">
    Move and scale images to fit the layout you want.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Backgrounds" icon="fill-drip">

  </Card>

## Upload a PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Import Designs">
    Go to [Designs](https://admin.pitchprint.com/designs) and click **Import Designs**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/66ec338dceae9468399d482c/file-UfpHq2RxDG.png" alt="Designs page in the admin with the Import Designs button highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Upload Design PDF">
    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/66ec33c3811a2434cb6d3d9c/file-io2ieB05sZ.png" alt="Import Designs options with the Upload Design PDF choice highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your file">
    Pick the PDF from your local storage. It is added as a design in the selected category, ready to edit and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  We are still adding functionality to the uploader. Send suggestions or questions to [support@pitchprint.com](mailto:support@pitchprint.com).
</Note>
