---
title: "BigCommerce user Guide"
description: "Install the PitchPrint app on BigCommerce and set up your first customizable product."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your BigCommerce account">
    If you don't have a store yet, sign up at BigCommerce and [start a free trial](https://www.bigcommerce.com/essentials/free-trial/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the PitchPrint app listing">
    Go to [PitchPrint on the BigCommerce App Marketplace](https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/pitchprint/?search=pitchprint) and click **Get this app**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install the app">
    Click **Install** and approve the requested permissions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in to PitchPrint">
    Log in to your PitchPrint account using the credentials emailed to the address on your BigCommerce account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your installation plugin">
    Select the installation plugin you intend to use.

    <Frame>
      <img src="https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/5f57b5344cedfd00176328a3/file-B7u7jDBt8M.png" alt="Selecting the installation plugin" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up your first product">
    With the plugin selected you can import designs, create a new design, assign assets and create a product.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  For the full walkthrough with screenshots at every stage, see [How to install PitchPrint on BigCommerce](/documentation/how-to-install-pitchprint-on-bigcommerce).
</Note>
