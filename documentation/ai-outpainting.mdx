![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/672c7da377f27013219ac405/file-ggzFImaODN.png)

This module allows your customers to expand images beyond their original borders using AI outpainting technology. By leveraging powerful machine learning algorithms, outpainting analyzes the style, textures, and composition of an image to seamlessly generate new content that blends with the existing elements. This technique can be used to:

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/6718e9aa3a0b506883e52870/file-ohxW14eROa.jpg)

\- **Expand a Scene:** Reimagine a cropped photo by predicting and filling in what the rest of the scene might look like.

\- **Creative Edits:** Enable artists and designers to extend artwork, create variations, or develop larger visuals from smaller sections of their designs.

\- **Enhance and Repair Images:** Restore incomplete or damaged images by filling in missing parts with coherent visual elements.

**How to use Outpaint:**

Start by uploading your image into the PitchPrint editor, and inserting it on the canvas.

Once the image is on the canvas, click on the 'Edit Picture' button

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/671a02a04d8d375180ed10d4/file-tu4Q6yn3Rq.png)

In the side panel you will find the AI Image enhancement tools, Click on 'outpaint'

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/671a0893c8770a0e6b658311/file-YdNKdkrtme.png)

The AI Feature will then automatically expand the image for you.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/helpscout.net/docs/assets/58f1ec6d2c7d3a52b42f8254/images/671a08c13a0b506883e5295a/file-cRTDxYiuYZ.png)
